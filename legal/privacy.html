Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ankidote is a study app for medical students. This page explains what information the app collects, what we use it for, who else handles it, and the choices you have. Using the app is also covered by the Terms of Use.

Who we are

ankidote is run by Malak Elsadek, based in Egypt. Contact: emmykades@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, your name, email address and profile picture. If you use an emailed link, your email address.
  • Your study data. Decks, questions, folders, review history, statistics and settings such as theme, sound and zoom.
  • Your slides. The PDF, the text of each page and a cover image of the first page.
  • Generated questions, with their explanations and the slides they came from.
  • Question reports. When you tap "Report it" on a question, we save the question, the reasons you picked and any note you write.
  • Plan and usage. Your plan status, free-period end date, renewal date and how many AI questions you have used this month.
  • On your device. The app keeps your sign-in session and some preferences in your browser's local storage.

We do not collect your phone number, location or contacts, and the app has no advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your decks and progress in your account, on all your devices.
  • To write questions from the slides you choose.
  • To apply free-period and plan limits, and show your usage.
  • To read question reports and improve the quality of generated questions.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to fix problems.

We do not sell your information.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your slides

Slides are kept in private storage that only your account can open. Only upload material you have the right to use.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When an account is deleted, we delete its details, decks, review history, slides and reports. Copies in routine backups can remain for a short time before they are overwritten.

Your choices

  • Sign out any time in Settings.
  • Delete a deck, a folder or a slide file in the app, which also removes its slides.
  • Email us to delete your account and data, or to ask what we hold about you.
  • Clear the app's local storage in your browser at any time. You will be signed out.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and each account's data is kept apart by access rules in the database. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Children

ankidote is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Not medical advice

Questions written by the AI can be wrong. Always check them against your course material. ankidote is a study aid, not medical advice.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when payments start. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will tell you in the app about any significant change.
