faq

frequently asked questions

what is medidote?

a study app for medical students. upload lecture slides, let AI write exam-style questions from them, and review with spaced repetition. your slide shows beside each question.

does the AI make things up?

every question comes from your slides and keeps a quote from the source, so you can check it. it can still make mistakes, so use the report button when something looks wrong.

how does the founding price work?

the first 30 people to subscribe pay 350 EGP a month and keep that price for as long as they stay subscribed. everyone after pays 500 EGP.

what happens after the 7 days?

you keep your decks, slides and stats. AI question generation needs a paid plan.

is my data private?

yes. your decks and slides are only yours, and you can delete your account and all its data in settings. read the privacy policy.

which devices does it work on?

any device with a browser. you can install it to your home screen on iPhone, Android, iPad and laptops.

can i cancel any time?

yes. everything is month to month and you keep access until the end of your cycle.
